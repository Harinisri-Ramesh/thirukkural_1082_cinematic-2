## The Kural

*திருக்குறள் 1082 · காமத்துப்பால் · களவியல்*

	⁠நோக்கினாள் நோக்கெதிர் நோக்குதல் தாக்கணங்கு
	⁠தானைக்கொண் டன்னது உடைத்து.

*Meaning:*

அவள் பார்த்தாள்; நான் பார்த்தேன்; அந்த எதிர்ப்பார்வை ஒரு படையுடன் வந்து தாக்குவது போல இருந்தது.

 A man looked at her. She looked back at him. That unexpected return glance struck him with the force of an attacking army. Her look is small, but its impact is immeasurable.

## About the Project

This project reimagines Thirukkural 1082 as a modern cinematic story. A simple exchange of glances between two college students in a sunlit corridor is used to express the Kural's comparison between a powerful gaze and the impact of an attacking army.

The central idea is the *power of reciprocal eye contact*, shown through golden light, eye glow, floating particles, slow motion and a heartbeat-like pulse. The "weapon" is purely metaphorical: there is no violence anywhere in the project.

The visitor experiences the story first (he saw, she noticed, their eyes met), then the Kural is revealed, then its meaning and its link to today: *Story → Emotion → Gaze → Impact → Kural → Meaning → Modern Connection.*
